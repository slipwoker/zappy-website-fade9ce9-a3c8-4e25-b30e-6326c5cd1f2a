Serving Neighbors with Dignity & Compassionate Care

Hands Of Grace Community Outreach provides practical, life-sustaining support to individuals and families in Manning, South Carolina. From food and clothing distribution to assistance with overdue utilities and medications, we meet urgent needs with love and respect. While our work is rooted in faith, we welcome everyone — faith-based support is offered only upon request, ensuring an inclusive, judgment-free space for all.

Practical Support, Delivered with Dignity

We believe that everyone deserves access to basic necessities without shame or stigma. Our team works directly with local families to provide food, clothing, and financial relief for overdue utility bills and essential medications — all in a welcoming, respectful environment.

Our Services

How We Serve Our Community

We provide practical support with love, respect, and dedication. Every service is offered freely and without judgment — because everyone deserves a helping hand.

Food Assistance

We provide nutritious food packages to individuals and families facing food insecurity. Our distribution includes fresh produce, pantry staples, and culturally familiar items that honor the dignity of every household.

Who may benefit: Anyone in Manning and surrounding areas experiencing food hardship — no questions asked, no proof of income required.

Clothing Support

Our clothing closet offers gently used and new garments for all ages — from everyday wear to interview-ready attire. We believe clean, well-fitting clothing restores confidence and opens doors to opportunity.

Who may benefit: Families, job seekers, and individuals rebuilding after hardship. Seasonal items and children's clothing are regularly available.

Utility Bill Assistance

We help with overdue utility bills to keep the lights on and water running. Our team works directly with local utility providers to apply payments efficiently, preventing service disconnection during difficult seasons.

Who may benefit: Households facing a temporary financial crisis, seniors on fixed incomes, and families with past-due notices.

Medication Help

We provide emergency assistance for essential prescription medications. Whether it's a one-time need or ongoing support, we collaborate with local pharmacies to ensure no one goes without the medicine they depend on.

Who may benefit: Uninsured individuals, those between coverage, and anyone facing an unexpected medication expense they cannot afford.

All services are provided with dignity and respect. Faith-based support is available upon request — but never required.

How to Reach Out for Support

We believe that asking for help should be simple, dignified, and free of barriers. Whether you need food, clothing, or help with a utility bill, our process is designed to welcome you with warmth and respect. Here is how our community outreach works.

1

Reach Out

Contact us by phone at 803-225-3553 or visit our location at 1173 James Martin Road in Manning. A friendly team member will listen to your situation and guide you through the next steps. No complicated forms — just a conversation.

2

Share the Need

Tell us what kind of assistance would help most — groceries, clothing, overdue utilities, or medication support. We keep your information confidential and treat every request with the dignity and compassion you deserve. Faith-based encouragement is available if you request it, but never required.

3

Receive Support Guidance

After we understand your needs, we connect you with available resources — whether that is a food box, a clothing voucher, or direct assistance with a bill. Our team works hard to ensure you leave with practical help and the reassurance that you are not alone.

Need to speak with us directly?

Call 803-225-3553 during our office hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. If you reach us after hours, please leave a message and we will return your call as soon as possible.

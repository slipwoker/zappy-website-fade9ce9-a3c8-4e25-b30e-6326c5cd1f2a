Meeting Practical Needs with Dignity & Respect

We believe that everyone deserves access to life's essentials, delivered with warmth and unwavering compassion. Our core services are designed to lift burdens and restore hope, one neighbor at a time.

Food Assistance

Nutritious food packages and fresh produce distributed weekly to ensure no family in our community goes hungry.

Clothing Support

Gently used and new clothing for all ages, distributed with care to preserve dignity and meet seasonal needs.

Utility Help

Emergency assistance with overdue utility bills to keep the lights on and water running for families facing hardship.

Medication Assistance

Critical support for prescription medications and healthcare access, ensuring health is never compromised by financial strain.

A new organization built on dependable local care.

Hands Of Grace Community Outreach was born in Manning, South Carolina, with a simple but powerful mission: to serve our neighbors with love, respect, and unwavering dedication. We believe that every person deserves access to food, clothing, and essential support — and we are committed to providing that care with dignity, every single day.

“We serve our community with love, respect, and dedication — because dignity is not a privilege, it is a right.”

— Hands Of Grace Leadership

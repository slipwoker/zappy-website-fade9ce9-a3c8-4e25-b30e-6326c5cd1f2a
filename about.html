Our Story

Serving Manning with
Love, Respect & Dedication

Hands Of Grace Community Outreach was born from a simple yet profound belief: that every person deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion, and unwavering respect. Founded in Manning, South Carolina, we are a newly established faith-based nonprofit organization dedicated to walking alongside our neighbors during their times of greatest need. Though our roots are fresh, our commitment runs deep — planted firmly in the rich soil of this community we are honored to serve.

Our work is practical and hands-on. We distribute food and clothing to families facing hardship, assist with overdue utility bills to keep lights on and homes warm, and help provide access to essential medications for those who cannot afford them. Every service we offer is delivered not as charity from a distance, but as an extension of family — with listening ears, open hearts, and a profound belief in the inherent worth of every individual who walks through our doors.

Rooted in faith, open to all. We are a faith-based organization, not a ministry — spiritual support is available only upon request, and everyone is welcomed exactly as they are.

Our Mission & Values

Hands Of Grace Community Outreach provides practical, compassionate support to our neighbors in Manning, South Carolina. We believe in serving every person with dignity, offering help without barriers, and building a community where everyone feels seen, valued, and cared for — regardless of background or circumstance.

Dignity

Every person who walks through our doors is treated with the honor they deserve. We see the whole person — not just their circumstances — and provide help that preserves self-worth and fosters hope.

Respect

We honor the unique stories, backgrounds, and beliefs of everyone we serve. Our faith-based support is always offered by request — never imposed — ensuring a welcoming space for all.

Compassionate Commitment

We show up consistently with food, clothing, utility assistance, and medication support — not as charity, but as neighbors caring for neighbors. Our dedication is unwavering because our community deserves nothing less.

Our Leadership

Guided by faith, driven by compassion — meet the dedicated team serving the Manning community with love, respect, and unwavering commitment.

Ronald Collins

Founder & Managing Director

Ronald Collins established Hands Of Grace Community Outreach with a vision to bridge gaps in the Manning community. With a heart for service and deep local roots, he oversees strategic direction, program development, and community partnerships. His leadership ensures every initiative is carried out with dignity, transparency, and genuine care for those in need. Ronald believes that a helping hand, offered with grace, can restore hope and strengthen the entire community.

What You Can Expect from Hands Of Grace

We are dedicated to serving our community with love, respect, and unwavering commitment. Here's what that looks like for you and your neighbors in Manning.

Rooted in Manning

We live and work right here in the community we serve. Our deep local presence means we understand the real needs of our neighbors — from food insecurity to keeping the lights on — and we respond with timely, compassionate care that makes a tangible difference close to home.

Practical, Dignified Care

We provide food and clothing distribution, assistance with overdue utility bills, and help with essential medications. Every service is delivered with dignity and respect — you are not a case number here, you are a valued member of our community, and we meet you where you are without judgment.

Welcoming & Inclusive Support

While our work is inspired by faith, we are not a ministry. Faith-based support is available only upon request. Our doors are open to everyone in need, regardless of background or belief. You will always find a warm welcome, a listening ear, and a team ready to walk alongside you on your journey forward.

Extending hands of hope and healing throughout Manning and beyond. Rooted in faith, growing in community.
